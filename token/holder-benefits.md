# Holder benefits

DREMT does two things for a holder. It unlocks benefits at partner jewellers, and it pays for their pieces at a discount.

## 1. Holder codes

Any wallet holding DREMT, with no minimum and no lock-up, can generate one personal code for each partner.

1. Open [platform.dremt.io](https://platform.dremt.io) or the Telegram bot [@dremt_wallet_bot](https://t.me/dremt_wallet_bot).
2. Sign a short message with your wallet. This proves ownership. It is not a transaction and costs nothing.
3. Receive your code for the partner you chose.
4. Show the code at the partner. The partner verifies it at platform.dremt.io/partners/verify, and the Issuer re-checks at that moment that your wallet still holds DREMT.

Each code is single use. Codes do not expire. Holding more DREMT does not give a different code or a bigger benefit.

## 2. What the code unlocks

Each partner sets its own holder benefits. They include exclusive or limited pieces, early access to new collections, private viewings, and member discounts or perks. Ask DREMT support which benefit a partner currently offers.

## 3. Paying in DREMT

Partner pieces can be paid for at the DREMT Checkout:

- **In DREMT**: the partner's price is converted at the fixed primary price of 0.80 USD per DREMT, less the partner's pay-in-DREMT discount. The discount rate is set per partner and shown at checkout.
- **In SOL**: open to anyone, at the partner's full price.

The Issuer receives the payment and settles with the partner. Partners do not hold DREMT.

## What DREMT never does

Every benefit is a cheaper or better purchase. DREMT pays nothing out: no cashback, no yield, no staking return, no revenue share, no redemption, no buy-back. Nothing accrues to a balance, and no benefit grows with the size or duration of a holding. DREMT is not an investment.
