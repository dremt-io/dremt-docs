# Compliance & legal

## Classification

DREMT is a **utility token**. It is held for holder benefits at partner jewellers and spent at the DREMT Checkout. It is not backed by any asset, has no reference value, pays no yield, cashback or revenue share, and gives no right of redemption, buy-back or governance over the Issuer. No benefit depends on how much or how long a wallet holds.

## Terms

The DREMT Token Terms v1.1 (effective 13 November 2026) apply to every primary purchase from the Issuer, to holding DREMT and to the holder-benefit programme. In plain words:

- DREMT is a utility token, not money, not an investment, not a security.
- Primary issuance is on-chain at a fixed 0.80 USDC per DREMT. No refunds after minting.
- Holder benefits and pay-in-DREMT discounts are reductions or extras on goods and services. Nothing is paid out.
- The token program is controlled by one person, the Issuer's founder, through a 3-of-5 multisig, and can be upgraded.
- The Issuer does not support the price and does not promise liquidity.

## Undertakings given by the Issuer

Recorded in Schedule D of the Compliance Assessment: no redemption, buy-back, price-support, market-making or guaranteed-return arrangement exists; no funds have been taken for any yield or equity product; the dormant backing, oracle and stablecoin code paths will never be enabled; listing venues are notified before any program upgrade; no price, return or holding-size messaging is published.
